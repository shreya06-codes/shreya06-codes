## 👩🏻‍💻 About Me

Hey, I'm Shreya Rai!

I'm a Computer Science and Information Technology undergraduate at
Symbiosis University of Applied Sciences, Indore.

I'm interested in understanding how intelligent systems work and how
computing can become more efficient, reliable, and useful in the real world.

My journey into research began with exploring adaptive CPU scheduling
for heterogeneous multi-core systems. It taught me to look beyond
writing code and think about the problems, trade-offs, and questions
behind a computing system.

Today, I'm exploring the intersection of **AI, computer systems,
and responsible technology** through research, software projects,
and continuous learning.

- 🔬 **Research interests:** Computer systems, scheduling, AI and responsible computing
- 💻 **Building:** Software projects and algorithmic solutions
- 🧠 **Exploring:** Machine learning and data-driven problem-solving
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** DSA, programming, and machine learning
- 🤝 **Interested in:** Research collaborations and meaningful technical projects

I believe good engineering starts with asking better questions,
testing ideas carefully, and learning from what doesn't work.

### Adaptive Remaining-Time-Aware Process Scheduling for Heterogeneous and Energy-Constrained Multi-Core Systems

My first research paper explores process scheduling in heterogeneous,
energy-constrained multi-core environments, with a focus on remaining-time
awareness and adaptive scheduling decisions.

**Research focus**
- Remaining-time-aware scheduling
- Phase awareness and heterogeneous cores
